{% comment %} The "All seasons" view of a show's Episodes section (Task 130): a four-figure readout of the whole run, then one row per season. It is what a reader scans to answer "is this show worth it, and which seasons?" before drilling into a season's episodes. Every number comes from movies/season_stats.py, which owns the rules: an average is the mean of rated episodes only (unrated ones are left out, never counted as 0), and specials stay out of the show-wide figures. Averages print two decimals, single episodes IMDb's one: a mean carries the extra digit, and at one decimal four seasons of a steady show all read "8.5" -- which would make "best season" look arbitrary. A season name links to that season's panel. Without JS the link is a plain in-page jump to the stacked panel (each carries id="episodes-season-N"); with JS, initSeasonPicker() in theoria.js selects the season instead.
