Follows the season slicer — pass `show` for the "All seasons" view, or `row` for one season. Every number comes from movies/season_stats.py, which owns the rules: an average is the mean of rated episodes only (unrated ones are left out, never counted as 0), and a season resting on fewer than three ratings is never named best or weakest while seasons with a real sample exist. Averages print two decimals, single episodes IMDb's one: a mean carries the extra digit, and at one decimal a steady show's seasons all tie. A best/weakest season name links to that season's panel: a plain in-page jump without JS; with JS, initSeasonPicker() (theoria.js) selects it.
